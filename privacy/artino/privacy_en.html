Artino Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Artino is a local zine-making app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information You Provide

Artino works with the photos or image files that you choose to import. Imported images are used to create editable zine pages, previews, exports, and locally saved projects.

Local Processing

Image analysis, layout generation, editing, preview rendering, and export rendering are performed on your device. Artino does not upload your imported images to our servers and does not use cloud processing in the current version.

Photos, Files, and Exports

  • On iOS and iPadOS, Artino requests access only when you choose photos to import or save exported images or videos to Photos.
  • On macOS, Artino reads and writes files only through files or folders you select through the system file picker or save panel.
  • Exported PNG, JPG, PDF, long image, spread, and video files are created only when you choose to export.

Purchases

Artino Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Artino may store a local entitlement state, product identifier, and subscription expiration date on your device so the app can unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

Settings Stored On Device

Artino stores app preferences such as language and appearance locally on your device. These preferences are used only for app functionality.

Tracking and Analytics

Artino does not track you across apps or websites and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current version.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your imported images, local projects, or app preferences with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Artino is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as Artino changes. If a future version adds accounts, cloud features, analytics, or other data handling, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
