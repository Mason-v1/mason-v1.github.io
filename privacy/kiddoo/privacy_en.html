Privacy Policy

Kiddoo · Updated October 9, 2026

Kiddoo helps families save and organize children's artwork. This policy explains how the App handles information when you use photos, camera, voice recording, iCloud sync, and purchases, and how you can manage that information.

In brief: Artwork is stored in the App's local storage by default. Related artwork leaves the device only when you enable iCloud sync, use a legacy iCloud Drive backup, or choose to export or share it.

1. Scope and users

This policy applies to the Kiddoo iOS App. It is intended for parents and guardians to record children's artwork. An adult should decide whether to add a child's name, photos, or voice and whether to enable cloud sync. The App can create a local guest profile and does not require a child to register a separate online account. You may use a nickname for the artist.

2. Information handled by the App

  • Artwork: Images you choose or capture, generated thumbnails, titles, descriptions, categories, artist names, creation dates, ratings, frame styles, and voice recordings you choose to make.
  • Local profile and settings: A local user profile, language and appearance preferences, iCloud sync status, and information needed to manage sync.
  • Purchases: Apple's StoreKit handles purchases and restoration of premium features. The App uses transaction status to determine entitlement. Apple processes payment details; the App does not directly receive card information.
  • Contact information: If you email the developer using your mail app, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send so we can respond.

Please avoid putting unnecessary addresses, school names, contact details, or other sensitive information in artwork titles, descriptions, or recordings.

3. Permissions and purposes

Camera access is used when you choose to photograph artwork. Photo access or the system photo picker is used to import images you select. Microphone access is used only when you choose to record audio. Denying a permission limits that feature but does not prevent you from viewing saved artwork. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

The App uses this content to show and edit your artwork timeline, play recordings, apply frames, and, after you enable sync, save artwork in your iCloud account. We do not use children's artwork to build advertising profiles or sell artwork content.

4. Local storage

Artwork images, thumbnails, and recordings are kept in the App's local sandbox. Artwork details and settings are kept in local device storage. Deleting an artwork in the App removes its local record and associated files. Uninstalling the App normally removes its local sandbox data; device or system backups may retain separate copies according to your iOS settings.

5. iCloud sync and legacy backups

After you choose to enable iCloud sync, artwork details, images, thumbnails, and recordings are stored through Apple CloudKit in your Apple Account's private iCloud database and synced to devices using the same account with this feature enabled. Sync requires an available iCloud account and network connection; offline changes may complete later. The App does not upload artwork to a server operated by the developer.

Turning sync off on one device does not automatically remove content already uploaded to iCloud. When sync is enabled, deleting artwork in the App queues deletion of its cloud record, which takes effect after a successful sync. Before switching Apple Accounts, uninstalling, or troubleshooting a sync failure, make sure you have a copy of artwork you want to keep.

You can manually create or import legacy iCloud Drive backups. Those backups are managed separately from CloudKit sync. Deleting local artwork or disabling CloudKit sync may not delete old backups. Apple manages iCloud accounts, storage, and availability.

6. Sharing and third-party services

When you choose to export artwork through the system share sheet, you select the recipient. Copies shared elsewhere are subject to the recipient service's practices. Apple provides StoreKit purchases and restoration, and iCloud sync and backup. If you open the developer website or send email, the relevant website or mail provider may handle visit or communication data. The App currently does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics services.

7. Access, retention, and deletion

You can view and edit artwork in the App and delete individual works from their menus. To remove all local App data, you can uninstall the App after checking your backups. Content already synced to iCloud, legacy iCloud Drive backups, device backups, and copies you shared with others may need to be managed or deleted in their respective locations.

For a privacy request you cannot complete in the App, contact us using the address below. We may need limited information to identify the issue. Please do not send unnecessary photos of children or a complete artwork library.

8. Children's information

Parents and guardians should manage children's artwork information and access to the device. You can use a nickname instead of a real name. Before adding photos or voice recordings, consider whether they are appropriate to store or sync. If you believe a child's information has been handled incorrectly, contact us so we can review what we can address.

9. Security and changes

The App uses the iOS sandbox and Apple's iCloud capabilities to store the information described here. Protect your device and Apple Account, and handle exported files carefully. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. If the App's data handling changes materially, we will update this policy and its date.
