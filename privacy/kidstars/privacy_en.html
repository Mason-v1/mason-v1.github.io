Privacy Policy

KidStars · Effective October 1, 2026

KidStars is a family star chart for parents and children. We care about children’s privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and how parents can manage it.

In brief: Family data stays on this device by default. It leaves the device when a parent enables iCloud family sync or chooses to export or share a backup. The parent PIN stays in this device’s Keychain and is not included in family backups or iCloud family sync.

1. Scope and users

This policy applies to the KidStars iOS app. It is designed for families. A parent creates and manages children’s profiles, tasks, rewards, and records. Children are not asked to create separate accounts or provide real names; parents may use nicknames and built-in avatars.

2. Information handled by the app

  • Child profiles: Nickname, age group, and emoji avatar entered by a parent. If a parent chooses an image through the system photo picker, the selected and cropped avatar image is also stored.
  • Family rules and records: Earning and deduction tasks, rewards and star costs, completion and deduction history, redemption and fulfillment status, timestamps, star balances, badges, and sticker progress.
  • Preferences and security: Selected child, language and appearance settings, iCloud sync state, and the parent PIN.
  • Messages you send us: If you tap “Contact developer” and send an email, we receive the address, text, and attachments you choose to send so we can respond.

The current app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavior analytics SDKs. We do not sell family data or use it for targeted advertising.

3. How information is used

The app uses this information to show profiles and avatars, calculate stars, record tasks and rewards, display badges and stickers, verify the parent PIN, create family backups, and sync and share family data when enabled by a parent. We do not use children’s records to build advertising profiles.

4. On-device storage and parent PIN

Family data is stored in app files on this device; avatar photos are stored as separate local image files. Language and appearance are stored using local system preferences. The PIN is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device. Family backups and iCloud family sync do not transfer it to other family members. Please protect your device with a passcode.

5. iCloud sync and family sharing

After a parent enables iCloud family sync, child profiles, tasks, rewards, star and redemption records, and selected avatar photos are stored through Apple CloudKit in that Apple account’s iCloud database for syncing across devices. The app uses Apple’s private and shared databases. It does not send family data to a server operated by the developer.

If a parent invites family members, invited people who accept can access and change shared family data. The parent selects recipients and their Apple accounts through Apple’s sharing flow. Invite only people you trust. Offline changes may remain on the device until a later successful sync.

Apple provides and manages iCloud accounts, transmission, storage, and availability. Turning off iCloud on a device or uninstalling the app does not itself delete copies already held in iCloud or on other invited devices.

6. Photos, backups, and external actions

You may select a child avatar with the iOS photo picker. The app receives only the image you select, not your entire photo library. You may use an emoji avatar instead.

“Export family backup” creates a JSON file containing family data and avatar images and passes it to the destination you choose in the system share sheet. The app does not separately encrypt the exported file. Please store, share, and delete backups carefully. “Restore from backup” reads a file you select and replaces the current local family data with it. When iCloud sync is enabled, the app does not permit a backup to overwrite a shared family directly.

Opening the developer website, rating the app, or contacting the developer opens a website, Apple App Store, or your mail app respectively. Those services may process access or message data under their own policies. To locate the app’s App Store page, KidStars sends its bundle ID to Apple’s iTunes lookup service; it does not send family records.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete a child, restore another backup, clear app data, or uninstall the app. In Parent controls, you can delete a child’s profile; this also removes the corresponding local tasks, rewards, history, redemptions, and avatar. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletion enters the sync queue and reaches iCloud and other shared devices after the device reconnects and sync succeeds.

Backups you exported to files, sent to others, or saved on other devices must be deleted in those locations separately. Retention of iCloud data and system backups also depends on Apple’s services and device settings. Contact us below if you need help with a data request.

8. Children and parental choices

A parent decides whether to enter a child’s nickname, age group, or photo, enable iCloud sync, and invite family members. Using a nickname and emoji avatar can reduce personal information. Parents can review, change, export, and delete family data in the app. A parent or guardian should make requests relating to a child’s data.

9. Security and changes

We use iOS app sandboxing, Keychain, and CloudKit for the data described above. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure; keep your system updated and protect your Apple account and exported backups. If the app’s data practices change materially, we will update this policy and make the new version available in the app.
