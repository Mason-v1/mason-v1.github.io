MirrorMirror Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

Welcome to MirrorMirror (hereinafter referred to as “the App”). MirrorMirror is a local lipstick try-on and makeup cabinet tool. We care about your privacy and keep the core experience on your device.

1. Data We Process

  • Camera frames: used only for live try-on preview and lip positioning.
  • Photos you select: used only to render the current photo try-on result and save it if you choose to.
  • Cabinet items: brand, shade, finish, notes, and favorite status that you add.
  • Language preference: used to remember whether you selected Chinese or English.

2. Local Processing

The current version does not upload your camera frames, photos, or cabinet data, and it does not call external makeup APIs from the app.

Try-on rendering, shade matching, and cabinet management are handled locally on your device.

3. System Permissions

  • Camera permission: used for live try-on.
  • Photo library permission: used to choose try-on photos.
  • Photo library add permission: used to save try-on results.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include ad SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or sell/share personal data with third parties.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete cabinet items inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored runtime data.
