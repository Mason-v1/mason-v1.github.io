LumaLock Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Welcome to LumaLock (the "App"). LumaLock helps you create iPhone Lock Screen Live Photos from selected videos or multiple photos. We care about your privacy and keep the core creation process on your device.

1. Data We Process

  • Videos you select: used only to trim, extract frames, and generate a Live Photo.
  • Photos you select: used only to generate a photo-sequence Live Photo.
  • Generated exports: saved to the Apple Photos app only when you choose to export.
  • Local settings: such as language preference, export quality, first install date, free trial count, and Pro entitlement status, used to provide app functionality and restore your experience.

2. Local Processing and Media Files

The current version processes selected videos and photos locally on your device. The App does not upload your selected media to our servers for Live Photo creation.

Video trimming, frame extraction, photo-sequence rendering, and Live Photo packaging are handled on device. Your original photos and videos remain in your photo library unless you delete them in the Apple Photos app.

3. System Permissions

  • Photo library permission: used to choose photos or videos for Live Photo creation.
  • Photo library add permission: used to save generated Live Photos, videos, images, or GIFs.
  • Camera permission: used only if you choose to record video content inside the App.

4. Local Storage and Keychain

The App may use UserDefaults or Keychain on your device to store preferences, free trial count, an on-device generated identifier, first install date, and Pro entitlement status. Some Keychain data may remain available to iOS after the App is uninstalled, which helps prevent repeated trial resets or restore entitlement state.

This information is used for local App functionality and is not used to track you across apps.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not sell your selected media to third parties.

  • Apple StoreKit: used to process in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we do not access your full payment details.
  • Apple system services: photo library access, saving, and review prompts are provided by iOS system features and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete generated Live Photos or other exported files in the Apple Photos app. Uninstalling the App removes most locally stored runtime data. If you need help clearing local entitlement or trial data that may remain in Keychain, contact us. App Store purchase records are managed by Apple.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
