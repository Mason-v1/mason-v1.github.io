# Ocre 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-09-28

## 简体中文

Ocre 是一款在设备本地处理照片的创作应用。我们不要求注册账号，不在应用中接入广告或分析服务，也不运营接收你照片的服务器。

### 照片与画作

你主动选择的照片仅用于在设备上预览、渲染和导出效果。应用内置的参考画作和风格迁移模型也在设备上使用。Ocre 不会把照片上传到开发者服务器，不会用你的照片训练模型。如果所选原图只存于 iCloud，Apple 的照片服务可能先将原图下载到你的设备。

### 权限、保存和分享

选图组件可能请求读取照片图库的权限。只有你选择保存成品时，Ocre 才请求向照片图库添加图片的权限。你选择分享时，成品会交给 iOS 分享界面及你选择的目标应用。照片权限可以在 iOS「设置」中更改。

### 本机设置和购买

语言、外观偏好及是否提示过评分保存在设备本机。Pro 购买与恢复由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用读取经验证的购买权益以决定导出权限；开发者不建立账号或自有交易服务器。Apple 对购买信息的处理适用其自身政策。

### 反馈及外部链接

点击「反馈」会打开设备上的邮件应用。只有你决定发送邮件后，邮件地址、正文及你主动添加的附件才会发送给开发者，邮件也会由你的邮件服务商处理。点击馆藏、模型、开发者网站或 App Store 链接会离开 Ocre；对应服务可能依其政策处理访问数据。

### 联系与更新

如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 [zhyl1881@163.com](mailto:zhyl1881@163.com)。如果应用的数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策及应用内说明。

## English

Ocre is a photo creation app that processes images on your device. It requires no account and currently includes no advertising or analytics service. The developer does not operate a server that receives your photos.

### Photos and paintings

Photos you select are used on device to preview, render, and export effects. Bundled reference paintings and style transfer models are also used on device. Ocre does not upload your photos to the developer's servers or use them to train models. If an original is stored only in iCloud, Apple's Photos service may first download it to your device.

### Permissions, saving, and sharing

The photo picker may request access to your photo library. Ocre requests permission to add an image to Photos only when you choose to save. If you choose to share, the result is passed to the iOS share sheet and the destination you select. You can manage photo access in iOS Settings.

### Local settings and purchases

Language, appearance, and review prompt state are stored on your device. Apple StoreKit handles Pro purchases and restores. Ocre reads verified purchase entitlements to decide export access; the developer does not create an account or operate a transaction server. Apple's own policies govern its handling of purchase information.

### Feedback and external links

Feedback opens a mail app on your device. Your email address, message, and any attachments you add reach the developer only if you choose to send them; your email provider also handles the message. Museum, model, developer website, and App Store links leave Ocre and may be governed by those services' policies.

For privacy questions, email [zhyl1881@163.com](mailto:zhyl1881@163.com). If the app's data handling changes, we will update this policy and the in-app explanation.
