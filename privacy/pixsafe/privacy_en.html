PixSafe Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Welcome to PixSafe (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). PixSafe helps you create iPhone Lock Screen Live Photos from selected videos or photo sequences. We care about your privacy and keep the core creation experience on your device.

1. Data We Process

  • Videos you select: used only to trim and generate a Live Photo.
  • Photos you select: used only to generate a short photo-sequence Live Photo.
  • Generated exports: saved to your photo library only when you choose to export.
  • Language preference and purchase status: used to remember app settings and Pro access.

2. Local Processing

The current version processes selected videos and photos locally on your device. The App does not upload your selected media to a server for Live Photo creation.

Video trimming, frame extraction, photo-sequence rendering, and Live Photo packaging are handled on device.

3. System Permissions

  • Photo library permission: used to choose photos or videos for Live Wallpaper creation.
  • Photo library add permission: used to save generated Live Photos.
  • Camera and microphone permissions: used only if the App provides recording features that need them.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your selected media with third parties.

5. Data Deletion

Original photos and videos remain in your photo library. Generated Live Photos can be deleted from the Photos app. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored runtime data, while App Store purchase records are managed by Apple.

6. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
