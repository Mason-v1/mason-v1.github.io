Privacy Policy

PixSafe is designed for local photo privacy processing. Your selected photos are processed on this device.

Local Processing

The app scans selected photos for metadata, location information, faces, QR codes, and visible text using on-device frameworks. Photos are not uploaded to a server for the core cleaning experience.

Clean Copies

Exports are generated as new clean copies. Original photos are not overwritten. Clean copies may remove metadata and may include visible masks selected by you.

Data Collection

The core app does not require an account and does not use advertising or tracking SDKs. If analytics or crash reporting is added later, it should be disclosed before release.
