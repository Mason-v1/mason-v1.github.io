Stronger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-23

Local First

Stronger v1 does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync. Exercises, sets, weights, durations, share backgrounds, and preferences are stored locally on your device by default.

Photos and Camera

When you choose a share background, take a photo, or pick an image from Photos, the app reads only the image you select. Images are used to compose workout share cards and are not uploaded to a server.

Data Export

You can export local workout data as JSON from Settings. You decide where to save, share, or delete the exported file.

Third-Party Data Sources

The exercise library primarily references free-exercise-db, with source and license notes kept inside the app. Any expanded source pipeline will be reviewed for licensing before release.

Contact

If you have privacy or data handling questions, a feedback entry will be added in a future version.
